MY FAVORITE POEM

"Keep Moving Forward"

When the road is long and wide,
Keep your dreams close by your side.
When the journey feels so tough,
Believe in yourself and be brave enough.

Every step can make you strong,
Keep moving forward all along.
Learn from every passing day,
And never let your dreams fade away.

About the Poem

This poem is about perseverance, courage, and determination. It reminds us to continue moving forward even when life becomes difficult. We should believe in ourselves and continue working toward our dreams.
