Who can visit the pantry?

Anyone in our neighbourhood who could use a little extra support is welcome. We don’t ask for proof of income, a referral, or paperwork.

Do I need to book ahead?

No booking is needed to visit during open hours. If you’d like us to have a basket ready, you can reserve items on this site and choose a pickup window.

How much do groceries cost?

We keep prices low, with everyday pantry staples starting under $1. If cost is a barrier, please contact our team—we’ll do our best to help.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

We do our best to offer a range of choices, including vegetarian and gluten-free options when available. Item details and allergens vary by donation, so please check labels and ask our team if you have questions.

How can I donate food or money?

We welcome unopened, unexpired pantry staples and fresh produce. Monetary gifts help us fill gaps and buy what neighbours need most. Visit our support page for details.

Can I volunteer with you?

Yes, absolutely. We have opportunities in food sorting, pickup days, deliveries, and community events. Send us a note and we’ll find a good fit together.
