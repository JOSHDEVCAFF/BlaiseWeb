Our neighbourhood, nourished

Good food brings us together.

Neighbourhood Pantry began with a simple idea: when neighbours look out for one another, a community grows stronger. What started as a few shelves and a handful of volunteers has grown into a welcoming place to find groceries, share a meal, and feel at home.

We work with local growers, shops, and generous neighbours to make fresh, useful food available to everyone—without judgement and at no cost.
