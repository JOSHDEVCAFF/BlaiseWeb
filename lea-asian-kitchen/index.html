Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Can I add dishes to my basket?+

Yes. Use the menu filters and Add to basket buttons. The sample basket is saved in this browser.

Can I place a real order here?+

No. This is a static student website demo. It does not send an order, arrange delivery, or process payment.

Are the menu prices final?+

No. Menu items and PHP prices are examples. Confirm actual dishes, ingredients, availability, and pricing with Lea.

What payment methods are accepted?+

No real payment is accepted on this demo. The payment section is informational only and does not request payment details.

Where is the kitchen based?+

The concept is based in Mandaue City, Cebu, Philippines. No street address or delivery radius has been provided.
