Lea Tapdasan is the heart behind Lea's Asian Kitchen. Based in Mandaue City, Cebu, and studying at MCC, Lea created this little food-shop concept to share the Asian dishes she loves.

From a cosy bowl of noodles to a plate made for sharing, there's something here for every kind of craving.
